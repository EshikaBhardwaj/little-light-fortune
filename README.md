# Little Light Fortune

MASTER PROMPT: THE DIWALI FORTUNE EXPERIENCE

A Synergos festive digital experience

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Transform the existing Diwali Fortune application into an exceptionally premium, immersive and interactive Diwali experience created by Synergos.

This experience will be accessed through a QR code printed on a collection of five physical Diwali postcards, which can be gifted to clients, colleagues, friends and loved ones.

The objective is to create a memorable physical-to-digital interaction.

When someone scans the QR code, they should immediately feel that they have entered something special, something that has been thoughtfully designed and created exclusively for Diwali.

The experience should combine:

- The warmth and magic of Diwali.

- The anticipation of a tarot-inspired fortune-picking ritual.

- Cinematic animation and immersive sound design.

- Beautifully art-directed typography and visual storytelling.

- A personalised fortune that users can download and share.

- A meaningful introduction to Synergos and its creative work.

The overall experience should feel like a premium interactive festive campaign by a leading creative agency.

01. CREATIVE DIRECTION & ARTISTIC APPROACH

The visual language should be contemporary Indian luxury.

Think of an intersection between:

- Indian festive traditions.

- Luxury editorial design.

- Contemporary tarot-inspired compositions.

- Cinematic motion graphics.

- Premium physical stationery and gold-foiled invitations.

Avoid making this look like a conventional Indian festival website.

Do not use excessive decorative elements, random mandalas, generic Diwali illustrations, cartoon graphics or unnecessary gradients.

Every visual element should have a purpose.

The experience should have a strong visual identity, distinctive compositions, beautiful typography and carefully controlled negative space.

Colour palette

Use a rich, sophisticated Diwali-inspired palette:

Colour	Hex code	Application

Deep Burgundy	#4B101D	Primary background

Festive Maroon	#701F2D	Secondary backgrounds

Antique Gold	#C6A15B	Borders, details

Champagne Gold	#E8D5A5	Highlights

Saffron	#D88A32	Festive accents

Vermilion	#B9412E	Selective highlights

Warm Ivory	#FFF7E9	Typography and cards

Midnight Brown	#281915	Dark contrast

Gold should feel metallic, reflective and luxurious rather than flat yellow.

Introduce occasional warm saffron and vermilion accents to retain the unmistakable Diwali character.

The overall composition should feel rich without becoming visually heavy.

02. TYPOGRAPHY & VISUAL HIERARCHY

Typography is one of the most important elements of this experience.

I want a strong editorial typography system with a noticeable oomph factor.

Do not make everything minimal, small or understated.

Create a distinct hierarchy:

Display typography

- Large, expressive serif typography.

- Elegant high-contrast letterforms.

- Beautiful proportions and generous spacing.

- Use for primary headlines and fortune reveals.

Secondary typography

- Contemporary sans-serif.

- Medium weights.

- Clean and highly legible.

- Use for instructions, supporting messages and navigation.

Decorative typography

- Small uppercase labels.

- Carefully tracked lettering.

- Used for category names, section identifiers and subtle brand details.

Create beautiful typographic compositions with intentional line breaks.

For example:

YOUR

DIWALI

FORTUNE.

The typography should occupy space confidently.

Use a combination of oversized headlines, small supporting copy and carefully positioned decorative labels.

Maintain generous margins and whitespace.

Do not compress content to fit the screen.

Every screen should have a clear focal point.

The experience should feel like a beautifully designed editorial campaign, not a dashboard or application template.

03. SCREEN 01 — THE MAGICAL ENTRY

The user scans the QR code.

Do not immediately show the fortune-picking interface.

Create an atmospheric opening sequence.

Start with a nearly black, deep burgundy screen.

A tiny golden spark appears in the centre.

The spark moves slowly across the screen, leaving behind a fine trail of light.

As it moves, it begins revealing a delicate Indian ornamental pattern, almost like a gold-foil illustration being drawn in real time.

The pattern should be sophisticated, intricate and restrained.

Gradually, a small golden diya emerges in the centre.

Introduce the headline:

SOME LIGHT

IS MEANT TO FIND YOU.

Supporting copy:

"This Diwali, a little surprise is waiting to unfold."

A small instruction appears:

"Take a moment. Make a wish. Let the light guide you."

The diya should be the primary visual element.

Diya animation — extremely important

I want this to be the signature moment of the experience.

Do not simply make the diya glow or fade in.

Create an actual lighting ritual.

1. A tiny spark travels towards the diya wick.

2. The wick catches fire with a realistic ignition animation.

3. The flame grows naturally, with subtle movement and variation.

4. A warm golden light begins illuminating the diya.

5. The light gradually spreads across the surrounding surface.

6. Fine golden particles begin floating upwards.

7. A beautiful circular light formation develops around the diya.

8. The light expands to reveal the next interaction.

Use layered animation, realistic lighting, depth, subtle shadows and carefully controlled particle movement.

The flame should feel organic rather than like a static icon.

Include a subtle sound of a match striking, followed by a soft flame ignition and a delicate Indian-inspired ambient musical texture.

Sound must be optional, with a visible mute/unmute control.

The sequence should feel cinematic, like the opening of a beautifully art-directed festive film.

04. SCREEN 02 — THE FORTUNE RITUAL

Once the diya is illuminated, transition into the fortune-picking experience.

The diya should remain part of the visual environment, perhaps smaller and positioned towards the bottom of the screen.

Introduce the headline:

A LITTLE LIGHT.

A LITTLE DESTINY.

Supporting text:

"Some wishes are written. Some are waiting to be discovered."

Now introduce five beautifully illustrated fortune cards.

The cards should be arranged in a visually engaging composition.

Think of premium collectible cards inspired by traditional tarot compositions, reinterpreted through Indian festive symbolism.

The five cards:

1. THE SUN — New Beginnings

2. THE LOTUS — Growth & Flourishing

3. THE STAR — Dreams & Possibilities

4. THE ELEPHANT — Wisdom & Good Fortune

5. THE DIYA — Light & Happiness

Card design direction

Each card should have:

- A distinct central illustration.

- Intricate but restrained ornamental detailing.

- Antique gold borders.

- A sophisticated colour composition.

- Beautifully balanced typography.

- A consistent visual system across all five cards.

The illustrations should feel like premium hand-crafted artwork, not generic AI-generated clip art.

The cards should have a slightly dimensional appearance, with realistic shadows and subtle gold foil detailing.

Card interaction

When the user enters this screen:

- The five cards should emerge from the golden light.

- They should arrange themselves into a beautiful spread.

- Introduce subtle depth and perspective.

- Each card should have a gentle idle animation.

- On tapping a card, it should respond with a slight physical movement.

- The selected card should move forward and become the focal point.

Display:

PICK A CARD.

DISCOVER WHAT AWAITS.

Instruction:

"Trust your instinct. Choose the one that speaks to you."

The user must be able to select any of the five cards.

Do not reveal the fortune immediately.

Build anticipation.

05. SCREEN 03 — THE CINEMATIC FORTUNE REVEAL

This should be the emotional highlight of the experience.

Once the user selects a card:

1. The selected card separates from the deck.

2. It moves towards the centre.

3. The surrounding cards gradually disappear into the background.

4. The selected card rotates with a smooth 3D flip animation.

5. Its ornamental border begins illuminating.

6. A golden light spreads from its centre.

7. The illustration transforms into a glowing visual composition.

8. The fortune gradually reveals itself.

Use a combination of:

- 3D card transformations.

- Mask-based reveals.

- Light sweeps.

- Golden particles.

- Depth and shadow.

- Smooth easing.

- Layered motion.

Avoid ordinary fade-in transitions.

The reveal should feel like the user has unlocked something.

Fortune content

Each fortune should be unique and drawn from a curated database.

Create at least 100 original fortunes divided into:

- Love & Relationships.

- Growth & Success.

- Abundance & Prosperity.

- Happiness & Joy.

- Courage & Confidence.

- New Beginnings.

- Peace & Well-being.

- Creativity & Inspiration.

- Unexpected Opportunities.

- Gratitude & Fulfilment.

Example:

GROWTH & FLOURISHING

"Everything you've been nurturing quietly is preparing to find its moment in the sun. Trust the process. Your time to bloom is closer than you think."

Another example:

NEW BEGINNINGS

"An unexpected opportunity may lead you somewhere beautiful. Stay curious, stay courageous and never underestimate the magic of beginning again."

Keep the messages warm, meaningful, optimistic and beautifully written.

Do not make literal or guaranteed predictions about people's futures.

Fortune card layout

The revealed fortune should appear on a beautifully composed digital card.

Use:

- Oversized category typography.

- An expressive serif message.

- Delicate ornamental details.

- A distinctive central illustration.

- Beautiful margins and negative space.

The card should look good enough for someone to save as their phone wallpaper.

06. SCREEN 04 — KEEP YOUR FORTUNE

After the reveal, introduce:

A LITTLE SOMETHING

TO TAKE WITH YOU.

Supporting copy:

"May this little thought stay with you through the year ahead."

Provide three actions:

SAVE MY FORTUNE

Download the fortune as a high-resolution PNG, 1080 × 1350 px.

The downloaded card must retain the same visual identity, typography and design quality.

SHARE THE GOOD FORTUNE

Enable:

- WhatsApp sharing.

- Native mobile sharing.

- Copy link functionality.

The shared content should include the fortune and a link to revisit the experience.

DISCOVER ANOTHER FORTUNE

Allow the user to return to the card-selection screen and choose another fortune.

Prevent immediate repetition within the same session.

All buttons must be fully functional.

Keep the interaction elegant and uncluttered.

07. SCREEN 05 — THE SYNERGOS CONNECTION

This is a particularly important part of the experience.

I do not want the transition to Synergos to feel like an advertisement or an unrelated redirect.

The user has just experienced something beautiful.

Now we need to establish a natural narrative connection between that experience and the agency that created it.

The transition should communicate that the creativity, thought and imagination behind this little experience are what Synergos brings to brands.

After the fortune has been revealed and the user has had the opportunity to download or share it, gradually transition the screen.

Imagine the golden light from the fortune card travelling towards the edges of the screen.

The light begins forming a beautiful typographic composition.

Display:

EVERY POSSIBILITY

BEGINS WITH AN IDEA.

Supporting copy:

"From little moments of magic to meaningful brand experiences, we believe creativity can transform the way people connect with ideas."

Then introduce:

This little Diwali experience was imagined and created by Synergos.

Follow with:

"Curious about what else we create?"

Primary CTA:

EXPLORE SYNERGOS

Secondary text link:

"Discover our work →"

CTA functionality

The primary button must redirect to the actual Synergos website.

The portfolio/work link must redirect to the actual work or projects section.

Use these configurable URLs:

- Main website: https://www.synergostech.com/

- Work/Portfolio: https://synergostech.com/portfolio/

- About: https://www.synergostech.com/about-us/

Do not invent URLs.

If the website has a dedicated projects or case studies section, use that as the primary destination.

The transition should be smooth and intentional.

The user should understand that they are moving from the Diwali experience into the creative world of Synergos.

Important: Do not automatically redirect users. Let them consciously choose to explore the agency.

08. CINEMATIC MOTION & SOUND DESIGN

The quality of animation will determine whether this experience feels premium or generic.

I want the experience to have the polish of a high-end interactive campaign.

Prioritise:

- Realistic diya ignition.

- Beautifully animated flames.

- Light travelling across surfaces.

- Intricate ornamental line formations.

- 3D fortune card interactions.

- Layered transitions.

- Golden particle systems.

- Metallic foil reflections.

- Soft atmospheric lighting.

- Smooth scroll and screen transitions.

- Beautiful button interactions.

- Micro-interactions on touch.

Sound direction

Create an optional ambient soundscape.

Suggested sound moments:

Interaction	Sound

Initial entry	Soft atmospheric Indian instrumental

Diya ignition	Match strike and flame ignition

Diya lighting	Gentle temple bell

Card selection	Delicate metallic chime

Card reveal	Soft magical shimmer

Fortune revealed	Subtle uplifting musical accent

Synergos transition	Elegant ambient transition

Keep sound tasteful.

Avoid loud Bollywood music, excessive bells or cartoon-like effects.

Audio should be OFF by default, with an obvious control to enable it.

09. RESPONSIVE DESIGN & USER EXPERIENCE

This experience will primarily be accessed through QR codes on mobile phones.

Mobile experience is the highest priority.

Requirements:

- Design mobile-first.

- Support iOS and Android browsers.

- Ensure cards are easy to tap.

- Maintain beautiful typography on smaller screens.

- Prevent horizontal scrolling.

- Ensure animations do not compromise performance.

- Optimise image and animation assets.

- Ensure the experience loads quickly after scanning the QR.

- Include reduced-motion accessibility settings.

- Ensure downloadable cards render correctly on mobile.

The experience should also adapt beautifully to desktop and tablet screens.

10. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Build upon the existing Emergent project.

Do not discard or unnecessarily rebuild the existing fortune-generation functionality.

Recommended implementation:

- React for the interface.

- Framer Motion for transitions and interactions.

- CSS animations for lightweight effects.

- Three.js or equivalent only where genuinely needed for advanced 3D effects.

- Structured local fortune database.

- Canvas-based or equivalent high-resolution image export.

- Web Share API for native sharing.

- WhatsApp sharing fallback.

- Responsive layouts.

Use optimised animation techniques.

Do not sacrifice loading speed for unnecessary effects.

The QR should open the live experience directly without login, registration or additional steps.

Ensure the final website is deployed and all buttons and external links work.

11. FINAL ART DIRECTION — NON-NEGOTIABLE

The final experience must look like a professionally art-directed Diwali campaign.

I want a strong balance of:

FESTIVE: The colours, light, warmth and visual symbolism of Diwali must be immediately recognisable.

PREMIUM: Sophisticated typography, luxurious materials, rich colour combinations and beautiful compositions.

MAGICAL: The lighting ritual, card selection and fortune reveal must create genuine excitement.

EDITORIAL: Strong typography, confident layouts, excellent spacing and a distinctive visual hierarchy.

IMMERSIVE: Smooth cinematic transitions, atmospheric sound and layered animations.

BRANDED: Synergos should be discoverable through a meaningful narrative, not intrusive advertising.

Avoid:

- Generic AI-generated website templates.

- Excessive minimalism that removes the festive character.

- Flat yellow and gold colours.

- Cartoonish illustrations.

- Generic horoscope interfaces.

- Unnecessary gradients.

- Excessive floating particles.

- Bouncy or childish animations.

- Tiny typography.

- Crowded layouts.

- Placeholder buttons and non-functional interactions.

Every screen must feel intentional and beautifully designed.

THE EMOTIONAL TAKEAWAY

When someone scans the QR code, they should think:

"Wow. Someone actually created this beautiful little experience for Diwali."

And when they discover Synergos, they should understand that the same creativity and attention to detail is what the agency brings to its work.

Deliver a fully functional, deployed, responsive Diwali microsite with:

1. Cinematic Diwali opening.

2. Interactive diya-lighting experience.

3. Five beautifully illustrated fortune cards.

4. Interactive card-selection experience.

5. Animated fortune reveal.

6. At least 100 unique fortunes.

7. Downloadable fortune cards.

8. WhatsApp and native sharing.

9. Meaningful Synergos brand introduction.

10. Functional website and portfolio navigation.

11. Optional ambient sound.

12. Fully responsive mobile experience.

Build the complete experience, not just static screens or a UI prototype.

My additional creative recommendations

There are three things I'd particularly like you to consider while developing this.

1. Make the diya the signature visual asset.

Don't treat it as an illustration that sits on the screen. Make the entire experience revolve around its light.

The flame can be the visual transition between different stages. The diya lights, the light reveals the cards, the cards reveal the fortune, and the light eventually introduces Synergos.

This creates a consistent visual narrative rather than disconnected animations.

2. Give the fortune cards their own visual personalities.

I wouldn't make all five cards look identical with different illustrations. Maintain one design system but give each card a distinct composition, colour accent and ornamental identity.

3. Make the Synergos introduction feel like a reveal of its own.

Don't simply place a logo and a button at the bottom of the fortune page. Treat the introduction as the final chapter of the experience.

The brand should feel like the creator behind the magic.
